How It Works · The Autonomy Loop
One closed loop — from take-off to report, with no pilot

Every BBA deployment runs the same loop. A human sets the objective once; the system executes it repeatedly, on schedule, and only escalates to a person when something matters.

01 — FLY

Autonomous Flight

Take-off, route, obstacle avoidance and landing — handled by the on-board autopilot. Indoor, outdoor, GPS or no GPS.

→
02 — SEE

AI Vision

On-board / edge AI detects cracks, hotspots, defects, intrusions, assets, people and change — frame by frame.

→
03 — DECIDE

Real-Time Logic

Thresholds, zones and persistence filters turn raw detections into confirmed alerts — not noise.

→
04 — REPORT

Dashboard & Evidence

Geo-tagged findings, counts, heatmaps and clips land on a live dashboard and exportable report.

↺  Then the drone returns to its dock, recharges, and repeats — on a schedule, without a crew on site.
